Helpful answers

Before you book.

A few common questions about service intervals, pricing and what to tell the team.

LM Brothers recommends general servicing about every three months for a typical home. Usage, pets and the environment can affect how often a unit needs attention.
Published prices are $50 for one unit, $60 for two, $75 for three, $90 for four, $110 for five and $120 for six. The published prices have no GST added on top. More than six units and commercial systems are quoted separately.
The published diagnostic call-out is $50 for standard units and $120 for VRV/VRF systems. The standard diagnostic call-out is free if the team proceeds with the repair. Confirm the applicable fee when enquiring.
Share how many units you have, whether they are wall-mounted or ceiling cassette, what is happening, your area and whether the property is residential or commercial. A photo of the unit can also help.
The company states that it serves customers island-wide in Singapore, with no travel charge. Appointment availability depends on the schedule, so send your location to check.
LM Brothers Aircond lists its opening hours as every day, 8am to midnight, including weekends and public holidays.
