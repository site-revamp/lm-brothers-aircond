# LM Brothers Aircond Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

LM Brothers Aircond Pte Ltd provides air-conditioning servicing, maintenance, repairs, and installation for residential and commercial customers across Singapore. The homepage redesign aims to help visitors choose a service, understand pricing and warranty coverage, and request a booking.

## Main Improvements

1. **Clarify where to start.** The original homepage gives visitors little direction on choosing a service or requesting a booking. The redesign prioritises clear service choices and a prominent booking action.
2. **Make pricing and trust information easier to find.** Published rates, customer reviews, island-wide coverage, and the 90-day workmanship warranty need greater visibility. The redesign gives these details more prominence so customers can assess the service before enquiring.
3. **Connect service information to enquiries.** The original homepage presents services and contact options in a fragmented way. The redesign organises service information around customer needs and gives visitors a clear route from their chosen service to an enquiry.

## Design Improvements

The layout prioritises service selection, gives pricing and warranty details a clear place in the page hierarchy, and makes it easier to find the next step after reviewing a service.
